CODEV2E, short for Corporation of Delay Entertainment Vision 2 Entertainment, is an independent animation studio founded on May 15, 2026.

Based in Odisha, India, the studio exists with one simple purpose: to build original stories, characters, and worlds from the ground up.

CODEV2E does not operate as a commission-based production house. We build our own universes, character designs, and visual concepts.
